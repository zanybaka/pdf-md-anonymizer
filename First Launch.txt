First Launch on macOS
=====================

macOS blocks apps from unidentified developers by default.
The easiest fix is one Terminal command:

    xattr -cr "/Applications/PDF-MD Anonymizer.app"

Then double-click the app — it will open normally.


Without Terminal (macOS Ventura / Sonoma / Sequoia):

1. Double-click the app — macOS shows a "cannot be opened" alert. Click Done.
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the Security section — you will see
   "PDF-MD Anonymizer was blocked". Click "Open Anyway".
4. Enter your password if prompted.
5. Double-click the app again — click "Open" in the final dialog.

You only need to do this once.
