NOTE FOR MAINTAINERS / AI AGENTS
--------------------------------
This list must mirror what is actually shipped in the .app bundle. After any
dependency change (briefcase requires, transitive bumps), regenerate the
canonical list with:

    bash scripts/list_bundle_packages.sh

and reconcile any new lines into this file before tagging a release. The
release-flow checklist in DEPLOYMENT.md repeats this requirement.
